## Platform

web

## Users

The primary user is the warehouse keeper, working within an assigned warehouse to create and maintain operational documents, enter document lines, attach signed originals, submit work, and record count quantities.

Secondary users are:

- Warehouse managers, who review, reject, post, reverse, and supervise warehouse operations within their granted scope.
- Data managers, who maintain catalog, organization, and warehouse master data.
- Auditors, who inspect inventory, documents, assets, counts, reports, and immutable audit history without changing operational records.
- System administrators, who manage users, roles, and permissions.

Employees, organizational units, sites, and external parties may be recipients or custody holders without necessarily being application users.

## Product Purpose

The product gives the General Authority for Oversight and Inspection one controlled source of truth for inventory and assets distributed across sites and warehouses. It replaces scattered operational records with real-time warehouse balances, traceable asset responsibility, and auditable workflows while preserving the signed paper document as the legal basis for each stock operation.

Success means every balance can be explained by its movements, every movement can be traced to an official document, every asset's responsible party can be identified at any point in its history, and unauthorized or unrecorded stock changes are prevented.

## Positioning

The product is document-driven rather than balance-entry-driven: stock cannot be edited directly. Receiving, issuing, transfer, opening balance, counting, and adjustment operations flow through official documents and append-only movement history, with the required signed original connecting the internal government paper process to the digital audit trail.

## Operating Context

The product runs as an internal government, on-premises web application for the General Authority for Oversight and Inspection. It supports geographically distributed sites, organizational units, warehouses, and scoped Enterprise, Site, or Warehouse responsibilities.

Operational work follows formal warehouse documents and separation of duties. Warehouse keepers prepare and submit ordinary documents; warehouse managers perform the governed review and posting actions. The signed paper original remains the official legal reference and must be represented by the required attachment and verification workflow before posting.

The interface is Arabic-first and right-to-left. It must remain usable on desktop, tablet, and mobile layouts, with dense operational tables, forms, dialogs, document timelines, attachments, and reports.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The v1 product covers receiving, issue, atomic transfer, opening balance, inventory count, adjustments and disposal, assets, custody, roles and permissions, audit history, and reports.
- Only a warehouse holds inventory. Organizational units organize people and responsibility but do not hold stock balances.
- Materials are defined once in a central hierarchical catalog and become available to warehouses through domain-based warehouse capabilities.
- Every stock movement is generated by a posted document; balances are derived, explainable caches and are never edited directly.
- Stock movements, custody history, asset movement history, and audit records are immutable ledgers.
- Negative stock is prohibited. Issue and transfer operations must respect available balance and warehouse capability rules.
- Asset responsibility distinguishes operational responsibility from personal custody while preserving one traceable custody timeline.
- Authorization is permission- and scope-based. The frontend controls visibility and workflow guidance, while the API remains the authoritative policy and transaction enforcer.
- Access credentials and protected data must remain within the approved on-premises and same-origin security boundaries. Access tokens are memory-only; browser-managed refresh credentials are not exposed to JavaScript.
- The current name "EIAMS" is a working identifier, not a binding product or brand name. A permanent product name remains an open decision.

## Product Principles

1. Preserve the legal document trail: digital workflows strengthen the signed official record rather than bypassing it.
2. Make every quantity and responsibility explainable through immutable history.
3. Guide each user to the actions allowed by their role, scope, and workflow state without implying that the client replaces server authority.
4. Optimize the primary warehouse-keeper workflow for accuracy, speed, and clear recovery from validation errors.
5. Reuse shared operational patterns so equivalent documents, tables, forms, and policy states behave consistently across the Authority.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The product must be Arabic-first and fully RTL, using logical layout directions and Arabic user-facing copy. Keyboard access, visible focus, semantic labels, screen-reader announcements for dynamic feedback, sufficient color contrast, reduced-motion support, and responsive behavior across desktop, tablet, and mobile are required. Status and permission meaning must never rely on color alone.
